THE NEIGHBORHOOD CODE

How to play

Roll a six to let a rogue slip out of its hideout. Move clockwise around the grass path, then guide your crew up its colored lane and into the oak-tree center.

  • Tap a highlighted rogue to move it. You get another roll on a six.
  • Land on a rival to send them back to their house. Marked patches are safe.
  • Get all four rogues home first to win. Exact rolls are needed for the final step.

A playful, no-gore game of backyard skulduggery. The tiny knives are just cartoon props.
